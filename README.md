<h1 align="center">Country Explorer</h1>

<p align="center">
  A modern Android app to explore every country in the world with detailed information including population, area, capital cities, languages, currencies, and more.
</p>

---

## Features

- **All Countries** — Browse, search, sort, and filter 250+ countries
- **Country Details** — Population, area, region, capital, currencies, languages, timezones, borders
- **Favorites** — Star any country to save it for quick access
- **Pinned Countries** — Pin important countries to the top of your list
- **Compare** — Side-by-side comparison of two countries
- **Random Country** — Discover a random country with all its details
- **Country Quiz** — Test your geography knowledge
- **Rankings** — View countries ranked by population or area (highest/lowest)
- **Continents / Regions / Languages / Currencies** — Explore by category
- **Recently Viewed** — Quick access to your browsing history
- **Dark / Light Theme** — Midnight blue + teal + soft gold palette with system default support

## Tech Stack

- **Language:** Kotlin
- **UI:** XML Layouts, AppCompat, Fragments, Material Design
- **Networking:** HttpURLConnection + org.json
- **Async:** Kotlin Coroutines
- **API:** [countries.dev](https://countries.dev) (free, no auth required)
- **Flags:** flags.restcountries.com
